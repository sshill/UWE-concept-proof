1. The Historical Doctrine: Conceptual Precedent & Lineage

Yuriy Kondratyuk (1929, LOR & Orbital Fuel Depots) Paradigm

In his seminal 1929 work 'The Conquest of Interplanetary Spaces', Ukrainian aerospace pioneer Yuriy Kondratyuk (Oleksandr Shargei) formulated the Lunar Orbit Rendezvous (LOR) trajectory chosen by NASA for the Apollo program. Crucially, Kondratyuk established the concept of distributed orbital fuel depots and modular spacecraft decoupling, proving that space dominance relies on rendezvous logistics and staging nodes rather than single giant rockets.

2. The Bureaucratic Trap & Empirical De-Noising

Sci-Fi Free-Flight Tropes vs Keplerian Celestial Mechanics

Popular culture envisions space warfare as dogfights with free directional maneuvering. In physical reality, spacecraft are prisoners of gravity: orbital velocity at LEO is 7.8 km/s. Changing orbital inclination by just 15° costs ~2,000 m/s of ΔV—equivalent to burning 50-70% of total satellite mass in chemical propellant. Space combat is governed strictly by Keplerian two-body celestial mechanics and Clohessy-Wiltshire relative equations.
