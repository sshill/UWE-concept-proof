1. The Historical Doctrine: Conceptual Precedent & Lineage

Chumak Tabor (Wagenburg) & Cossack Mobile Field Fortresses

15th-18th century Ukrainian Chumaks and Cossacks organized heavy trade and military convoys into 'Tabors'—interconnected wooden freight wagons forming an impenetrable, self-defending mobile perimeter. In open terrain, the Tabor functioned as a modular, terrain-conforming armored ring capable of dynamic repositioning, defending against cavalry charges without static castle walls.

Calling military ground robotics 'vozyky' (carts) reduces MilTech to hobby gardening.
