"Articulated off-road robotic chassis require the IArticulatedChassis HAL contract to decouple central joint roll compliance from active yaw steering, eliminating tire slip scrub in deep mud."

🟢 Physical Superiority (Compliance with Law of Kinematics)

  • Articulated two-section frame provides passive mechanical roll conformity across rugged shell craters and trenches.
  • Eliminates skid-steer scrubbing in viscous mud, reducing drivetrain electrical energy consumption by 30-40%.

💡 Architectural Lesson for UWE: Demolishes marketing confusion: a passive articulating mechanical hinge is a chassis topology, NOT an adaptive suspension system.
