1. The Historical Doctrine: Conceptual Precedent & Lineage

Chumak Tabor (Wagenburg) & Cossack Mobile Field Fortresses

15th-18th century Ukrainian Chumaks and Cossacks organized heavy trade and military convoys into 'Tabors'—interconnected wooden freight wagons forming an impenetrable, self-defending mobile perimeter. In open terrain, the Tabor functioned as a modular, terrain-conforming armored ring capable of dynamic repositioning, defending against cavalry charges without static castle walls.

2. The Bureaucratic Trap & Empirical De-Noising

Amateur Slang ('Vozyk') & Motor kW vs Bekker Soil Mechanics

Calling military ground robotics 'vozyky' (carts) reduces MilTech to hobby gardening. Marketing claims boast '10-tonne towing' based on electric motor kilowatt ratings while completely ignoring Bekker-Wong soil mechanics: drawbar pull is bounded by soil shear strength (F_pull = A·c + W·tanφ), not motor torque. Without adequate ground contact area, high-torque electric motors merely dig wheels into viscous Ukrainian black soil (chernozem).

🟢 Physical Superiority (Compliance with Law of Kinematics)
