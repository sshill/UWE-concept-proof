### 5.3 The Algorithmic Mission Feasibility Engine
Prior to assigning an operational mission vector, the autonomous theater planner evaluates the **Mission Feasibility Index ($\Phi_{\text{mission}}$)**:

$$\Phi_{\text{mission}} = \mathcal{F}\Big(\mathcal{K}_{\text{medium}}, \mathcal{L}_{\text{container}}, \mathcal{E}_{\text{energy}}, \mathcal{C}_{\text{signal}}, \mathcal{T}_{\text{threat}}\Big)$$

Where:
1. **$\mathcal{K}_{\text{medium}}$ (Kinematic Medium Compatibility):** Evaluates real-time EAL tensors (soil shear vs. ground pressure, wave slamming $G_z$, wind shear, orbital drag).
2. **$\mathcal{L}_{\text{container}}$ (Logistical Availability):** Verifies the physical presence of required deployment infrastructure. If fixed runways in the sector are destroyed, all $C_4$ platforms automatically receive $\mathcal{L} = 0$.
3. **$\mathcal{E}_{\text{energy}}$ (Energy Budget):** Computes total mission watt-hours, including evasive sprint margins and required "Zhdun" loiter duration.
4. **$\mathcal{C}_{\text{signal}}$ (Electronic Warfare Resilience):** Determines communication survival probability along the attack axis. Under intense broadband jamming, only laser or fiber-optic platforms receive $\mathcal{C} \ge 1.0$.
5. **$\mathcal{T}_{\text{threat}}$ (Cost-Exchange & Attrition Calculus):** Evaluates whether an asset should be recovered or dynamically converted into an OWM kinetic ram.

$$\text{If } \Phi_{\text{mission}} \ge 1.0 \implies \text{Mission validated; token committed to memory; launch authorized.}$$
$$\text{If } \Phi_{\text{mission}} < 1.0 \implies \text{Mission blocked; autonomous planner selects alternative chassis-medium vector.}$$
