# Editorial Profile: Modern War Institute (MWI) vs Small Wars Journal (SWJ)

## 1. Modern War Institute at West Point (MWI)
- **Institution:** United States Military Academy (USMA), West Point.
- **Editorial Leadership:** John Amble (Editor-in-Chief), Charlie Faint (Managing Editor).
- **Core Audience:** U.S. Army leadership (TRADOC, Army Futures Command), field-grade officers (Captains to Colonels), West Point faculty & cadets, Joint Staff, NATO ground commanders.
- **Style & Length:** 600–2,500 words (Sweet spot: 1,800–2,200 words). Highly structured, intellectually rigorous, doctrinally grounded (FM 3-0, Multi-Domain Operations, Human-Machine Integration).
- **Institutional Weight:** Extremely high academic & doctrinal authority for Land Warfare. Articles influence Army doctrine, PME (Professional Military Education) curricula, and procurement priorities.

## 2. Small Wars Journal (SWJ)
- **Institution:** Independent platform under Arizona State University's Future Security Initiative (founded 2005 by Dave Dilegge & Bill Nagle).
- **Core Audience:** Operators at the tactical edge ('tip of the spear'), Special Operations Forces (SOF), Marine Corps (USMC), unconventional warfare practitioners, intelligence professionals.
- **Style & Length:** Flexible word count (typically 1,500–3,500 words). Punchy, provocative, practitioner-first ('discourse at the speed of relevance'). Hyperlinked citations embedded on action verbs/nouns.
- **Institutional Weight:** Legendary grassroots credibility in irregular, urban, asymmetric, and trench warfare. Free from bureaucratic Pentagon filters.
