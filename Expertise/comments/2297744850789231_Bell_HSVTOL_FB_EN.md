🟢 PHYSICS OF PARASITIC MASS & 1 CONVERTIBLE ENGINE:

1. Does parasitic mass make sense? In attrition warfare, NO. Tilt mechanisms, stop-fold blade hinges, and clutches add 25-35% deadweight to empty airframe mass. In jet cruise, folded rotors create massive drag (C_d0 > 0.025).

2. Does 1 convertible engine solve it? NO. Hover demands low jet velocity and high shaft torque, while 450+ knot jet flight requires high exhaust gas velocity. A 10,000 shp mechanical clutch weighs as much as a second engine and introduces fatal single points of failure.

🔴 UWE ENGINEERING VERDICT:
Trying to combine a heavy helicopter with a high-subsonic jet creates a compromised, inefficient platform on both ends of the envelope.
