🟢 AERODYNAMIC & PHYSICAL ADVANTAGES OF COAXIAL ROTORS:

1. Zero Anti-Torque Power Penalty:
Traditional single-rotor helicopters waste 12-15% of total engine shaft power simply driving the tail rotor to counteract torque. In a coaxial configuration, counter-rotating rotors cancel torque aerodynamically. 100% of engine power is converted directly into useful vertical lift.

2. Compact Operational Footprint:
A coaxial layout provides equivalent disc area with a 35% smaller rotor diameter compared to conventional tail-rotor helicopters. Eliminating the tail boom enables austere landings on frigate flight decks, confined forest clearings, and urban casualty evacuation sites without tail-strike hazards.

3. Symmetrical High-Speed Lift:
Retreating blade stall on one rotor is balanced by advancing blade lift on the opposing rotor, eliminating asymmetrical roll and enabling cruise speeds of 90-100+ knots with up to 10 hours of endurance.
