🔴 ENGINEERING & PHYSICAL DISCREPANCIES IN THE CGI CONCEPT:

1. Legged Robotics Energy Trap (Cost of Transport — CoT):
The Cost of Transport (CoT) for legged quadrupeds is 1.5–3.0, which is 5–10 times higher than tracked or wheeled chassis (0.2–0.4). A robot dog burns significant energy simply standing still to maintain dynamic balance. With an operational endurance rarely exceeding 3–5 km, without rapid automated docking and charging, the carrier quickly becomes an evacuation truck for dead batteries.

2. Ground Effect & Rotor Wake Interference During Mobile Launch:
Launching a heavy multirotor from the roof of a moving or vibrating carrier encounters severe ground effect turbulence and rotor wash reflection against the armored hull. Precision mechanical guide rails or magnetic hold-down locks are mandatory to avoid rotor tip strikes during deployment.

3. Electronic Warfare Vulnerability in Open RF Links:
The concept shows open radio links between the carrier and parasites. Under contested front-line electronic warfare (EW), unhardened 2.4/5.8 GHz links are jammed within dozens of meters. Real-world deployment requires high-level edge autonomy (VIO/SLAM without GNSS) or micro-tethered deployment options.
