🔴 SUSPENSION AUDIT: IS IT TRULY "ADAPTIVE"?
Stratonex claims an "adaptive" lever-arm suspension altering ride height (low for 70 km/h, high for mud).
This is not a high-bandwidth adaptive suspension (like active magnetorheological damping), but rather a quasi-static Active Variable-Geometry Suspension (AVGS).
Under a 500 kg load, each arm exerts extreme torque on actuator gearboxes. Calling it "adaptive" is marketing spin: exposed motorized pivot joints become failure points in deep mud.
