Analysis of the French KNDS CENTURIO UGV (SERA Engineering PHOBOS chassis).

A 2.8-tonne tactical platform combining 4-Wheel Drive with 4-Wheel Independent Steering (4WS) and hydrostatic propulsion.

🏷️ UWE CLASSIFICATION:
UGV: GL-DC-DR
(Wheeled Chassis | C2 Tactical Trailer | 60 kW Diesel JP-8 | ARX20/30 Turret | Fire Support | Resilient RF & Fiber)

🟢 4WS KINEMATICS:
Four-wheel independent steering (Ackerman, counter-phase, crab-crawl) completely eliminates the parasitic scrubbing friction of skid-steer UGVs. This saves 35-40% of traction energy and prevents tire tearing on hard terrain.

⚡ FIBER-OPTIC EW IMMUNITY:
Beyond 5+ km RF, KNDS integrates a hardened fiber-optic spool (Fibre Optique). In dense frontline EW, optical tethering is the only physical link enabling jam-proof 30mm remote fire control.

🔴 HYDROSTATIC VULNERABILITY:
High-pressure hydraulic circuits (300+ bar) are vulnerable to artillery shrapnel: a single puncture causes instant pressure loss and mobility kill, requiring ballistic skirts.
