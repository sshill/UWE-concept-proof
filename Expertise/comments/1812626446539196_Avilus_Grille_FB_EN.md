Cyber-physical engineering deconstruction of heavy-lift autonomous medical evacuation (CASEVAC/MEDEVAC) and frontline logistics rotorcraft under the Unmanned Warfare Engine (UWE) doctrine.


🔹 PHYSICAL BREAKTHROUGH & AUTONOMOUS CASEVAC

Avilus Grille resolves the central operational dilemma of modern combat casualty care: extracting wounded personnel within the tactical "Golden Hour" without exposing piloted helicopter crews to hostile MANPADS envelopes and FPV interceptors.

🟢 COAXIAL PROPULSION & PROPULSIVE REDUNDANCY: 12 brushless electric motors arranged in 6 coaxial pairs deliver 240 kW (~326 hp) of peak electrical power at 750 kg MTOW. Coaxial layout delivers hardware N-1 and N-2 failure tolerance: losing one or two motors/ESCs does not induce catastrophic yaw autorotation, enabling mission continuation or safe terrain recovery.

🟢 ENCLOSED CAPSULE & LIVE BIOMETRICS: Rather than exposing casualties on external litters, patients are housed in an internal 60-cm protected capsule shielded from ballistic fragments, downwash, and hypothermia (thermostatically regulated around 20°C). Integrated biomedical sensors transmit real-time ECG, pulse, and oxygen saturation to waiting trauma hospital surgical teams.

🟢 MODULAR C3 INTERMODAL CONTAINERIZATION: The complete platform, ground support, and critical spares fit within a standard 20-foot ISO sea container. Two soldiers assemble the airframe tool-free in under 15 minutes under a "Plug & Fly" operational protocol.


🔹 CRITICAL PHYSICAL CONSTRAINTS & TACTICAL TRADEOFFS

🔴 BATTERY ENDURANCE BOTTLENECK & RADIUS CEILING: Lifting 750 kg on pure chemical batteries imposes severe disc loading penalties. Operational radius is strictly bounded to ~25 km outbound + ~25 km return (51 km total mission profile) with 20-45 minutes flight time. Headwinds over 10 m/s or extended loading hovers rapidly deplete reserve safety margins.

🔴 ACOUSTIC & RADAR CROSS-SECTION EXPOSURE: A 7.8-meter rotor diameter with 12 spinning blades generates a massive acoustic footprint (audible from 2-3 km) and distinct micro-Doppler radar signatures. Operating at low altitudes and 86 km/h cruise speed presents a high-priority, vulnerable target for tactical air defense and FPV interceptors.

🔴 SEVERE ROTOR DOWNWASH ON AUSTERE SOIL: High disc loading drives ground downwash velocities exceeding 20 m/s. On dry dirt, sand, or snow, this induces severe "brownout" or "whiteout" conditions that blind optical terrain-relative landing sensors and jeopardize litter-loading ground crews.

🔴 IN-FLIGHT SURGICAL INTERVENTION DEFICIT: While telemetry enables remote monitoring, the lack of an onboard medic precludes active surgical or airway intervention if a casualty experiences tension pneumothorax or tourniquet slippage mid-flight.

🔹 ARCHITECTURAL LESSON FOR UWE

Avilus Grille validates the critical necessity of replacing multimillion-dollar crewed helicopters (UH-60 Black Hawk) with attritable autonomous heavy-lift rotorcraft for tactical casualty extraction in dense air defense bubbles. However, pure battery propulsion imposes a hard 25-km operational boundary. Scaling autonomous MEDEVAC to corps-level operational depths requires hybrid heavy-fuel turbine powertrains or Lift+Cruise aerodynamic wings to escape the disc loading energy penalty.
