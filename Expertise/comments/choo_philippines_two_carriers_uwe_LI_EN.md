In constrained archipelagic waters, screening depth collapses. Island terrain masking and narrow chokepoints compress engagement timelines to under 300 seconds. Open ocean enables long-range autonomous screening (USVs, UUVs, CCAs) while neutralizing adversary seabed sentinels. Deploying munitions from 5,000-meter abyssal depths imposes an insurmountable ascent latency penalty: by the time an effector surfaces, a fast-moving carrier has maneuvered dozens of kilometers away.

Geography dictates carrier survivability.
